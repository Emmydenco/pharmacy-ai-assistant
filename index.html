How it works

A simple path to the right kind of help.

Each interaction starts with a general question. The prototype is designed to be clear about what it can do—and when a person should take over.

Type a general question about using a medicine or understanding common information.

03+

About the project

Making everyday medication questions easier to start.

People often have practical questions about medicines but may not know whether to search online, wait for a pharmacy visit, or ask for urgent help. This student project explores a clearer first step for general information while keeping professional judgement with a pharmacist.

The aim is not to replace care. It is to learn how a small, safety-conscious assistant can communicate its limits, stop before unsafe advice, and make the next human step easier to understand.

Safety boundaries

The assistant should know when to stop.

Prompts and simple rules can support safer behaviour, but they do not guarantee clinical safety. This project remains subject to pharmacist, privacy, security, licensing, and regulatory review.

Suitable for this prototype

General information questions

For example: “What can I ask this assistant?” or “What is a general side-effect question?”

Needs a person

Diagnosis or treatment changes

The prototype will not diagnose, prescribe, change a dose, or recommend starting or stopping treatment.

Needs immediate help

Potential emergencies

Breathing trouble, collapse, seizure, overdose, or similar urgent situations should go to local emergency services.

Prototype status

A working foundation, not a finished clinical product.

The live demonstration includes a FastAPI backend, request validation, deterministic safety routing, mock mode, hosted-provider configuration, loading states, and safe provider failures.

Demonstration mode

Responses are simulated unless hosted inference is configured on the server. No conversation is saved and no real medication knowledge base is connected yet.
